Our Story

Built by a Marine.
Built to last.

DailyAdventureBox started with a simple frustration: people at the beach wanted to get on the water but didn’t own gear, couldn’t haul it, or couldn’t afford to rent it the old way. So we built a locker that lives where you play and opens from your phone.
