Our Story

Built by a Marine.Built to last.

A Marine’s idea, built in a garage, tested on a beach, contracted onto a military base, and now growing across the nation.

Why We Exist

Everyone deserves a day on the water.

At every base and beach, the same problem: people want to get on the water but don’t own gear, can’t haul it, or can’t afford to rent it the traditional way. DailyAdventureBox exists to erase that gap. Premium gear, where you already are, unlocked from the phone in your pocket.

“I built DAB because Marines and their families were missing out on the beach right outside their barracks. Gear rental was broken. So we fixed it with a locker that opens from your phone.”
Shay Putnam, Founder & Chief Executive

The Journey

From one locker to a growing network.

2019

The Idea & First Prototype

A smart locker that lives at the beach and unlocks from your phone. Sketched, then built by hand.

2021

Incorporated & Manufactured

Daily Adventure Box Inc. is formed and the first manufactured locker units are built.

2023

First Rentals

The first locker goes live on the beach, renting surfboards, paddle boards and beach gear to real customers. Patent application filed.

2024

The Rebuild

Real-world use exposed gaps. The whole system is rebuilt from scratch: new locker chassis, new app, new platform. Stronger, smarter, ready to scale.

2025

Government Contract

A concession contract is signed with the United States Marine Corps, the operational foundation for serving military communities.

2026

Operations Live & Growing

On-base and beach lockers operate side by side, with gear for every season on the way.

Next

Nationwide growth

Military installations, beaches, mountains, resorts, campgrounds and parks, each stocked with anything and everything its guests want.
