Reserve & Delivery

Reserve ahead.Unlock on arrival.

Tell us the day and the gear, and we’ll have it waiting. When you arrive, scan the code on the locker and finish in the free DAB app.

How to Reserve →Delivery

Where You Can Reserve

More lockers than the map shows.

Public locations are listed in the DAB app, with what’s inside each locker. They aren’t all of our locations: some lockers are private to the hotels, bases and communities they serve. Staying at one? You can reserve there too.

How to Reserve

Three steps, then go play.

01

Email Your Request

Send the location, date and time, the gear you want and how many people to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. For delivery, add the drop-off address.

02

We Confirm

We check availability and reply with your confirmation and how to pay.

03

Arrive and Unlock

Scan the code on the locker. The free DAB app handles the waiver and opens the door.

Delivery

We bring it to you.

Delivery is by request, to drop-off spots we approve.

  • 2027 delivery area: from the Camp Lejeune area down to Wilmington, North Carolina.
  • Confirmed by email: the drop-off spot, timing and any delivery fee.

Questions

Reserving, answered.

Do I have to reserve?
No. Walk up and rent whenever a locker is open to you. Reserving holds your gear for a specific day.
What if my plans change?
Email us as soon as you know and we’ll update or cancel your request.
