The Opportunity

Not a feed ad. A moment.

Every locker is a billboard at the exact moment someone reaches for outdoor gear: phone in hand, weekend ahead.

  • A qualified audience. People renting boards and kayaks buy sunscreen, apparel, snacks and experiences. You’re reaching a behavior, not a demographic.
  • Offline to online. Every panel carries a custom QR code to your site, promotion or app, so the physical presence is measurable.

Real Example

BOTE × DailyAdventureBox

BOTE, a premium paddle board and water gear brand, wrapped our lockers in their branding. Every locker became a full brand experience: their logo outside, their gear inside, their QR code pointing renters to their site.

  • Full locker wrap with BOTE branding and lifestyle imagery
  • QR code panel linking directly to BOTE’s website
  • BOTE gear inside the locker for renters to experience first-hand

A Quarter Panel

Logo and QR code on a quarter of one side panel, shared with up to three other brands. Best for local businesses, restaurants and service providers near our lockers.

Panel Spot

Half Panel

Half of a full side panel with room for messaging and imagery. Shared with at most one other brand. Best for regional brands and resorts.

Full Wrap

The Whole Locker

Every panel, your brand. Lifestyle imagery, QR codes and, if you make gear, your products inside. Best for national brands and major sponsors.
