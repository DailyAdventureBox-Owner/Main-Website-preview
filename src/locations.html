Questions

Frequently asked.

What type of gear goes in the locker?
Premium adventure and recreation gear: paddle boards, kayaks, surfboards, snorkel sets, floats and hangout docks, beach chairs and shade, sports equipment and games. We curate the mix for your location’s environment and guest profile, and we can add almost anything your guests ask for.
How much will my property earn?
Revenue share is set per location based on expected traffic, location type and locker size. You’ll see clear numbers before anything is signed. No surprises.
Do I need to do anything after it’s installed?
No. We handle restocking, gear maintenance, cleaning between rentals and customer support. Your only job is having a spot for the locker.
What if gear gets damaged or stolen?
All equipment is covered by our commercial insurance, and renters accept our terms and a safety waiver at checkout. Damage, loss or theft is handled entirely by us. Your property carries no liability.
How much space does it take?
Our small-gear unit has a footprint smaller than a mini-fridge and works indoors or outdoors. Board lockers need a bit more room. We do a site walk before installation and confirm the right placement for your space.
Can the locker be private to my guests?
Yes. Private lockers are not shown in the public app map and are available only to your guests, members or residents.
What does the agreement look like?
Terms are worked out per location. Our standard agreement covers installation, revenue share, maintenance responsibilities, insurance and exit terms. It’s straightforward and written to protect both parties.
