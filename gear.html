Good to Know

Renter questions, answered.

How much does it cost?
Pricing is set per location and per item and is always shown in the app before you rent. Most gear rents by the hour, and many lockers offer day rates.
The locker at my hotel or base isn’t in the app. Can I rent it?
Yes, if you’re a guest there. Private lockers aren’t listed publicly. Scan the code on the locker to open it in the app.
Do I need to book ahead?
No. Walk up to any public locker, or a private one where you’re a guest, and rent what’s inside right now.
Where do I return the gear?
To the same locker you took it from. Put it back the way you found it and end the rental in the app.
What if something breaks or gets lost?
Normal wear is on us. Our rental terms and safety waiver, accepted before the door opens, explain damage or loss. Email support@dailyadventurebox.com and we’ll sort it out.
Can kids rent?
Accounts are for adults. Parents and guardians rent on behalf of their kids and are responsible for them, including life jackets and supervision on the water.
Is the gear clean?
Yes. Equipment is inspected, cleaned and maintained between rentals, and anything that doesn’t meet our standard gets pulled.
